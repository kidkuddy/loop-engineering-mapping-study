# Protocol amendment 02 — scope of criteria I4 and F2

**Date:** 2026-08-29, at the point the classification design was fixed.
**Status:** applied.

## What it changes

I4 ("full text was retrievable by the search date") and F2 ("full text
unobtainable") were written for the registered design, in which every screening
survivor was to be assessed at full text. Under that design a record whose text
could not be read could not be judged, so it could not enter the corpus.

The implemented design classifies every survivor from title and abstract and
assesses a random sample at full text. Under it, retrievable text is a
precondition for the *validation* stage, not for inclusion, because inclusion no
longer depends on reading the full text.

> **I4 (amended).** Retrievable full text is not required for inclusion. It is
> required for a record drawn into the full-text validation sample.
>
> **F2 (amended).** F2 excludes a record *from the validation sample* when its
> text cannot be obtained. It does not remove the record from the map, and such
> records are reported separately from records excluded on substantive criteria.

## Why it is recorded rather than silently applied

Leaving I4 unamended would have the manuscript assert that every included record
satisfied a criterion that a substantial minority demonstrably do not: full text
is retrievable for a reported subset of the included records, not all of them.
That is a contradiction between the stated criteria and the corpus, and it is
resolved by amending the criterion to match the design that was actually run.
